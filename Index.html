Executive Proposal · Bogotá

AI-Native Performance Creative Bootcamp

A four-day production sprint designed to create bolder, higher-performing advertising and validate a faster AI-native operating model—not to teach the team how to use AI.

4 onsite days 25 launch-ready creatives 10 testable hypotheses USH · LATAM · Brazil Claude + Agents + Higgsfield

Two objectives. One controlled pilot.

The bootcamp separates the business outcome from the operating-model outcome. Every other measure supports one of these two goals.

1

Performance

Create and launch 25 hypothesis-led ads to identify new creative Champions capable of moving CPL from approximately $80 toward the target of below $50.

Success signal: at least 5 creatives at or below $50 CPL, or a ≥35% improvement versus the market control.
2

Transformation

Validate an AI-native workflow that enables the existing team to produce higher-quality creative faster, with repeatable agents and Higgsfield production pipelines.

Success signal: 25 launch-ready assets in four days and a ≥40% reduction in production time versus the current process.
Important: the four onsite days produce launch-ready assets and a controlled test plan. CPL proof happens during the post-bootcamp media test—not inside the room.

What this bootcamp is not

The cost of flying the team to Bogotá is justified only if every onsite hour is used for business-critical creative work.

An AI training course
A tool demonstration

The working model

Humans own insight, taste and decisions. Agents accelerate exploration and structure. Higgsfield becomes the production engine. Performance closes the loop after launch.

Stage 1

Decode

Translate the pre-built performance pack into creative problems.

Stage 2

Hypothesize

Define the customer truth, change expected and reason why.

Stage 3

Concept

Build bold verbal and visual ideas across all creative roles.

Stage 4

Design

Turn concepts into scripts, first frames and storyboards.

Stage 5

Produce

Record, generate or combine human and AI production.

Stage 6

Approve

Review craft, performance logic and market relevance.

Stage 7

Test

Launch, eliminate, iterate and scale after the bootcamp.

Investment protection

The bootcamp uses staged authorization. Travel is approved against readiness; production is approved against Creative Cards; media scale is approved against results.

0

Readiness Gate

No travel without the performance pack, validated agents, trained users, Higgsfield templates and talent readiness.

1

Concept Gate

Only ten differentiated, business-relevant hypotheses receive development time.

2

Production Gate

No actor or final-generation spend without an approved Creative Card, cost and production route.

3

Scale Gate

Post-launch investment moves only to creatives that meet the predetermined decision threshold.

The deliverables are tangible, testable and reusable beyond the four-day event.

Learning Architecture

10 hypotheses, 25 Creative Cards, a controlled test matrix, decision rules and clear iteration owners.

Repeatable System

Validated Copy and Video Agents, reusable Higgsfield workflows, production-time data and a weekly operating model.

Post-bootcamp decision

The executive decision is based on performance and operating efficiency—not enthusiasm for the technology.

Scale

At least 5 creatives reach ≤$50 CPL or improve ≥35% versus control, while production becomes materially faster.

Iterate

Leading metrics and efficiency improve, but CPL has not yet reached the target. Run one focused iteration cycle.

Stop / Redesign

No meaningful improvement in performance, quality or production speed. Do not expand the model until corrected.

This is not an investment in AI training. It is an investment in a measurable creative operating model.

The team arrives prepared, uses AI from the first hour, leaves with launch-ready work and earns the right to scale only through business results.
